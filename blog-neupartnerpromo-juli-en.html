How does being a RINGANA partner actually work?

Honestly: it's not a life contract. There are no monthly minimums you have to hit. No inventory to store, no boss, no fixed hours. You start, you see whether it fits, and you decide for yourself whether to keep going.

The Business Booster Starterset costs €34. For that you get your branded materials, access to the RINGANA partner portal, vouchers and entry into the Fast Track bonus programme. That's it. No hidden fee after that, no annual subscription, nothing.

  • You recommend products you genuinely use and love
  • Your customers order directly through your shop, you don't store or ship a thing
  • You earn commission on your own orders, on your customers' orders, and on your team partners' turnover
  • No monthly minimum order, no inventory, no annual fee
  • The more you build, the more the income grows, even when you take a break

Our grandmother has been doing this since 1999. Ulli for over 25 years. I started at 18. Not one of us has given up a single thing that actually mattered to us for this.

The Fast Track Bonus, simply explained

Everyone who starts with the Business Booster enters the Fast Track programme automatically. Think of it as a bonus on top of your normal commission, paid monthly when you hit certain milestones.

How it works in practice

1

Months 1 to 4: Each month you hit the minimum goal (a combination of turnover and active partners in your team), you receive a fixed bonus on top of your normal commission. Hit more than the minimum, earn more than the minimum bonus.

2

Months 5 to 8: You get two jokers. If life happens and you miss the goal one month, you don't lose your place in the programme. You just don't receive the bonus for that month, and you pick up again the next.

3

After month 8: Fast Track ends automatically. Everything you've built stays. Your commission and team income continue as normal, with no expiry date.

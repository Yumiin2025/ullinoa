Who is becoming a RINGANA partner the right step for?

This is the question I get asked more than any other. And the honest answer is: it depends on what you're hoping for. I essentially see three types of people who join us, and all of them find what they were looking for.

1. You love the products and want to order more affordably

As a RINGANA partner you earn commission on your own orders, which comes back to you and makes your own products cheaper overall. The price on the product is the same — the difference is that a share of what you spend flows back to you as commission. No obligation, no minimum turnover. Some people stay at this level, and that's completely legitimate.

2. You share the products and cover your own orders

Many RINGANA partners earn enough through recommendations to their circle to cover their own monthly orders. No sales events, no catalogues. Just sharing what you genuinely already love.

3. You want to build a real side income with RINGANA

This is the path I take, and the one my mum has been walking for 27 years. An income that grows while you sleep. No inventory, no boss, no minimum volume. Side income with RINGANA that genuinely fits around your life instead of working against it.
